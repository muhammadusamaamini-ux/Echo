# Echo

A personal Spotify-style music player for Android.

- **Your library**: songs, playlists, shuffle, repeat, a queue, and a full-screen player. Music keeps playing with the screen off, with controls in the notification and on the lock screen.
- **Import from Google Drive**: tap **+ Import → Google Drive or phone files**, open ☰ → **Drive**, and select your songs (tap and hold to select many). They are copied into Echo, so they play offline.
- **All music on this phone**: adds the songs already on your phone.
- **YouTube playlists**: paste a playlist link (or share it from the YouTube app to Echo). The videos play in YouTube's own player inside Echo, with internet and the screen on. **Find these in my songs** makes an offline playlist from matching songs you have imported.
- **Search**: find songs in your library, or search Google and YouTube inside the app. On a YouTube playlist page, tap **Import playlist**.

## Getting the app
Every time the code changes, GitHub builds the app. Open **Releases** and download `Echo.apk` from the newest build. It installs over the old version and keeps your music.
